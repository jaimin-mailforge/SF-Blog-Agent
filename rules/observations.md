# Observation log

Real first-hand observations, recorded so the practitioner voice has true material to draw on.

**The rule this file exists to serve.** `rules/writing.md` section 7 forbids inventing any first-person specific with a number, a timing, or a personal-experience claim. It also requires two or three first-hand moments per article. Both hold only if there is somewhere real to get them. This is that place.

**How to use it.** Any sentence in a draft that makes a first-person claim with a number has to trace back to an entry below. If there is no entry, you have three options and inventing one is not among them: use category-general framing without the personal frame, cite a verified source, or make a broad tenure claim with no specifics. Or go run the test and add an entry.

**How to add an entry.** Date it, name the product and the exact thing measured, and record the number as observed rather than as rounded marketing. An entry with a vague number is worse than no entry, because it invites a writer to sharpen it.

## Entries

### OBS-001 | Salesforge mailbox scaling
- **Observed:** Connected 22 mailboxes to a single Salesforge account during testing. The bill did not change, because Salesforge prices on active contacts and email volume rather than per mailbox or per sender.
- **When:** August 2026
- **Verified by:** Jaimin, confirmed 2026-08-13
- **Reusable as:** the concrete proof for any no-per-mailbox-pricing argument. Use the mailbox count, not a rounded "dozens".
- **Careful with:** say the bill did not change for adding mailboxes. Do not extend it to "the bill never moved", which is an absolute claim and banned.

### OBS-002 | Instantly time to first campaign
- **Observed:** Signup to a live campaign in under 15 minutes. Bulk mailbox connection worked with no manual DNS step.
- **When:** August 2026
- **Verified by:** Jaimin, confirmed 2026-08-13
- **Reusable as:** the fastest-setup claim in any cold email comparison. It was the fastest of the eight tools tested.

### OBS-003 | Woodpecker cost at low volume
- **Observed:** 500 contacted prospects came to $35 per month. That included 8,000 emails, 2,000 stored prospects, 2 warm-ups, and 100 Lead Finder credits, with nothing upsold to reach it.
- **When:** August 2026
- **Verified by:** Jaimin, confirmed 2026-08-13
- **Reusable as:** the worked example for per-prospect pricing being cheap at low volume and expensive at scale.

### OBS-004 | Cold email tool test, eight tools
- **Observed:** Eight cold email tools tested over 90 days. Every tool got the same contact lists, the same sequences, and the same mailboxes, and was scored on inbox placement, real cost with mailboxes and warm up added, time from signup to live campaign, bounce catching, reply handling in one place, cost at doubled volume and headcount, whether email and LinkedIn run in one sequence, and whether mailboxes are included.
- **When:** May to August 2026
- **Verified by:** Jaimin, confirmed 2026-08-13
- **Reusable as:** the testing-methodology basis for the cold email software article and any piece drawing on the same test. The eight questions are the openly-stated selection criteria.
- **Careful with:** the 90 days and the eight tools belong to this specific test. Do not attach them to a different article covering a different tool set.
