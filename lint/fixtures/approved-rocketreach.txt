The way people find leads has changed.

It used to be a workflow: open the tool, pick "SaaS," pick "Series A to B," pick "United States," pick "Head of Marketing," pick "50 to 200 employees," hit search, wait, scroll, uncheck the ones that don't fit, export.

That's a filter stack. Every B2B contact tool built its UI around it for a decade because that's what buyers knew.

Then, everyone (including myself) got used to typing full questions into Google, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity through MCP and getting an answer back within the AI tool they use.

RocketReach still runs the filter stack without AI features such as MCP, CLI, etc.

It works, and for a lot of teams it still gets the job done. But it feels like the tool I used to use, not the one I'd pick today.

That's because bounces on "verified" emails, credits that burn on bad data, unused credits forfeited at cancellation, auto-renewals that fire without warning, and a single-source database that runs thin outside US tech.

Every RocketReach buyer eventually runs into at least one.

I've tested 15 RocketReach alternatives, evaluated each on several criteria, and shortlisted the top 9 options that fulfilled today's requirement for lead generation.

## 4 Reasons People Leave RocketReach in 2026

These four are the ones that come up in almost every conversation I've had with a buyer looking to switch.

### Contact Data Accuracy

G2's own topic rollup logs 115 mentions of "Inaccurate Data" alongside 111 of "Outdated Contacts" and 84 of "Outdated Information."

Buyers report inconsistent data accuracy and outdated contact data, not just occasional misses. One G2 review sums up the gap from the accurate data teams expect from a sales intelligence tool:

[G2 Screenshot]

### Per-Seat Cost at Scale

B2B contact data tools moved to pooled credits years ago because outbound teams have lumpy demand.

RocketReach still prices per seat. Pro runs $829 a year per user on annual billing. Enterprise starts at $6,000/year. So, the starting price is not the real cost.

### Single-Source vs Waterfall

RocketReach does not provide waterfall enrichment. It queries one database per lookup.

If the database misses or holds a stale record, there is no Provider B.

### Lost Credits and Auto-Renewal

Annual export credits also do not roll over.

RocketReach's refund policy](https://rocketreach.co/refunds) does not require advance notice of upcoming renewals, and refunds are explicitly disclaimed once a billing period starts.

All these are reported across G2, Trustpilot, and Reddit. The best time to switch is usually before an annual contract renews, since lost credits and auto-renewal terms compound the cost of waiting.

## How I Tested These RocketReach Alternatives

I ran every tool below through the five checks. Not a scientific benchmark, just the questions a real buyer needs answered before writing a check.
- **Find rate on a live ICP:** I pulled a test list of 100 contacts matching a specific ICP (heads of marketing at Series A to B SaaS companies in the US, 50 to 200 employees) and measured how many the tool returned with a verified email.
- **Bounce rate on send:** I ran the returned emails through a validator before sending, then noted how many hard-bounced when actually sent.
- **Pricing transparency:** I checked whether the tool publishes pricing on its live page, whether the annual toggle is honest, and what the real cost lands at once you add the features most teams need.
- **Credit behavior:** I looked at what happens to unused credits at month-end, at renewal, and on cancellation, and whether bounced lookups get refunded.
- **Sending the leads out:** I checked whether I could export cleanly into a sequencer or CRM without a CSV hassle.
- **AI features (MCP and CLI).** I checked whether the tool ships a public MCP server, CLI, or other AI agent integration so tools like Claude and Cursor can run search and enrichment directly from an agent's environment.

## 1 - Leadsforge

**Best for:** Best RocketReach alternative for GTM teams & agencies that want clean waterfall-enriched leads with AI-native features like MCP CLI and want to send them straight into multichannel (cold email](https://www.salesforge.ai/blog/cold-email) + LinkedIn) sequences, without paying per seat or exporting CSVs to run outreach.

**G2 Rating:** 4.5 out of 5

Leadsforge is a B2B lead finder. It has a 500M+ contact database, and you search it by typing what you want in plain English instead of clicking through a stack of filters.

That is why I put it at the top. No other lead finder on this list has all four of these in one product: chat-based ICP search, waterfall enrichment, Competitor Followers Search, and Local Company Search. And the credits roll over instead of burning at cancellation.

The main reason I moved off RocketReach is data freshness. B2B contacts decay at roughly 30% a year as people change jobs, companies rebrand, and phone numbers move. RocketReach queries a single database per lookup, so when a record goes stale between refreshes there is no fallback.

Leadsforge cascades through multiple providers per lookup, so even when one source has a stale record, another usually has the current one.

In addition, Leadsforge, on the other hand, seamlessly integrate with major CRM and email outreach tools like Salesforge](https://www.salesforge.ai/) multichannel outbound sequences.

### Leadsforge Key Features

- **AI Lead Finder:**](https://www.salesforge.ai/lead-finder) Type your ICP in plain English (job title, industry, location, revenue, headcount, keywords) and get verified emails, LinkedIn URLs, mobile numbers, and company details back. Standard filter options still available.
- **Company Lookalikes:** Feed Leadsforge a list of your best customers and it returns similar companies by industry, size, revenue, and tech stack.
- **Competitor Followers Search:** Pull the LinkedIn followers of a competitor's company page, filterable by job title, seniority, and geography. A cleaner intent proxy than a cold firmographic pull.
- **Local Company Search:** Find companies inside a specific geographic area (city, region, or country) and enrich them into a lead list. Useful for territory-based outbound and field sales.
- **Waterfall enrichment:** Cascades through multiple data sources per lookup and keeps falling back until it finds a verified match. Deeper email validation runs as a separate step and burns additional credits.
- **Intent Signals:** Five signal types (Hiring, Job Change, Acquisition, Funding, Investor). Every match ships with evidence from public sources. Hiring Signals currently US-only, more geos coming.
- **Free Chrome Extension:** Salesforge-branded email and phone finder, shared across the Forge Stack. Surfaces verified emails and mobile numbers on LinkedIn and pushes contacts into a Salesforge sequence. Lookups consume Leadsforge credits.
- **Integrations:** Native HubSpot, plus Pipedrive, Salesforce, Slack, Clay, Zapier, and webhooks. Leadsforge integrates with cold email outreach tools including Salesforge with one shared login.
- **Public API:** A Leadsforge API key generated in app settings covers search, enrich, and export for custom pipelines and data workflows.
- **MCP server and CLI:** Forge MCP server and CLI connect Leadsforge to Claude, Cursor, and other AI clients for chat-driven search and enrichment.
