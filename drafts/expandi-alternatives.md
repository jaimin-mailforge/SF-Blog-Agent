# 8 Best Expandi Alternatives for LinkedIn + Email 2026

**Meta:** Expandi costs $99 a seat, and $168 once you add the personalization it is known for. Here are 8 alternatives, priced properly.

---

I have run LinkedIn outreach across most of the tools in this category. Expandi is a good one.

Its campaign builder is the widest I have seen. Eleven campaign types, real if-then branching, and daily limits the tool refuses to let you exceed.

But most people searching for Expandi alternatives are not unhappy with the automation. They are unhappy with the bill.

The published price is $99 per seat per month, or $79 if you pay for the year. One seat means one LinkedIn account. You pay for the seat whether or not anything is connected to it.

Then there is the line its own pricing page footnotes. Image and GIF personalization, the feature Expandi is best known for, is not included. It runs through Hyperise, and the cheapest paid tier there is $69 per seat per month.

So the setup people are actually comparing against costs $168 per seat per month. I have not found a single article that prints that number.

I went through the live pricing page, the product docs and the G2 review data for every tool below. Eight made the list.

Most of the ones that did not fell down on the same thing. They do LinkedIn only, and email arrives later as a step rather than a channel.

## TL;DR: 8 Expandi Alternatives

- **[Salesforge](#salesforge)** runs email and LinkedIn as two channels of one branching sequence, with unlimited mailboxes and warm-up included. From $40 a month billed annually, or $48 monthly. [Start a 14-day free trial](https://app.salesforge.ai/signup), no credit card needed.
- **[HeyReach](#heyreach)** charges per LinkedIn sender and gives you unlimited human seats free. $79 per sender monthly, $63 on annual billing.
- **[Dripify](#dripify)** puts native email in the sequence on every paid plan, and it publishes its exact daily action limits. $59 per user monthly, $39 annually.
- **[Meet Alfred](#meet-alfred)** runs LinkedIn, email and X in one sequence, and has the cheapest annual entry here. $59 per user monthly, $29 annually.
- **[Waalaxy](#waalaxy)** is the cheapest way in if LinkedIn is all you need. €19 per user per month on annual billing.
- **[La Growth Machine](#la-growth-machine)** bills per sending identity rather than per person, so teammates cost nothing. €60 per identity monthly, €50 annually.
- **[lemlist](#lemlist)** spans the most channels in one sequence, and lemwarm is included on every plan. Email from $55 a month billed yearly. LinkedIn needs Multichannel at $87 per user yearly.
- **[Snov.io](#snov-io)** is flat-priced with unlimited seats and native warm-up. $39 monthly, $29.25 annually, plus $69 a month for each LinkedIn slot.

If you run one LinkedIn account and send nothing by email, Expandi is fine. Stay where you are.

## Why People Leave Expandi

Expandi holds a 4.2 out of 5 on G2 from 158 reviews, which is a respectable score. Look at the shape of it and something else shows up.

Fifteen of those 158 reviews are one star. That is 9.5% of the total. For comparison, Waalaxy sits at 0.5% and lemlist at 1.0%, so Expandi's share is roughly ten to nineteen times theirs.

Most Expandi users are happy. A hard tenth are not. Three things account for most of what I hear from the second group.

### The $99 Seat That Becomes $168

Expandi has one self-serve plan. Business runs $99 per seat per month, or $79 per seat if you pay annually, which the page markets as two months free.

That is the number every comparison article prints. It is not the number you pay.

Image and GIF personalization is the feature Expandi built its reputation on. It is also not included. The pricing page says the service "requires an additional cost to your existing subscription" and leaves it there.

That cost is Hyperise. Its cheapest paid tier is $69 per seat per month, which covers 5,000 image views and up to 5 active images. Video personalization needs the $149 tier or a separate Senspark integration.

Add it up and one seat with the personalization is $168 a month. On annual billing it comes to about $136.50.

Now scale it. Five seats on monthly billing is $495 for Expandi alone, and $840 once Hyperise covers all five.

You are still not at a volume discount. Expandi's Agency tier starts at ten seats and sits behind a Talk To Sales button with no published rate. The three-to-five-seat agency, which is most of this market, has no price to check.

### LinkedIn-First, Email Bolted On

Expandi sells multichannel. Read its own documentation and the order becomes clear.

There are eleven campaign types: Connector, Messenger, Builder, Open InMail, Paid Open InMail, Group, Event Invite, Mobile Connector, Inbound, Company Follow Invite and Recovery. Not one of them is an email campaign.

Email exists as a single step. You get an Email action and an "If email exists" condition, and both live inside the Builder campaign only.

The inbox tells the same story. Expandi's help centre describes a unified LinkedIn and Sales Navigator inbox. Email replies do not land there.

So you connect your own Gmail or Outlook mailbox, and you need IMAP rather than SMTP for reply detection to work at all. There is no email infrastructure and no warm-up anywhere in the product.

Expandi knows this. It publishes its own guide to running the email leg through Smartlead, and Smartlead is a named integration step inside the builder.

That is a reasonable way to build a LinkedIn tool. It is not multichannel, and if email is half your motion you will be paying two vendors and reconciling two inboxes.

### One Seat Per LinkedIn Account, Paid Whether You Use It Or Not

This one is in the help centre rather than the pricing page, and it changes the maths for anyone with a team.

A seat connects to one LinkedIn account at a time. Pricing is per seat. And you pay for every seat you have purchased, whether or not it is connected to a LinkedIn account.

Read that last part again if you manage clients. A churned client whose account you disconnected is still billable until you remove the seat.

Compare it with how the rest of this list works. HeyReach charges per LinkedIn sender and hands you unlimited human users.

La Growth Machine bills per sending identity and includes 25 teammates free on its middle plan. Snov.io charges flat and gives unlimited seats on every paid plan.

Expandi is the only tool here where headcount and LinkedIn accounts are the same billable unit, and where an idle unit still costs you.

## How I Evaluated These Expandi Alternatives

Feature lists will not help you choose here. All eight run LinkedIn campaigns and most of them send email too.

The differences that matter are structural, and they turn up in seven places. I scored every tool against these:

- **What the billing unit actually is.** Per user, per LinkedIn account, per sending identity, or flat. This is the biggest swing in what you pay at five seats.
- **Whether email runs in the same sequence.** Native steps with branching, or a handoff into another tool you also pay for.
- **Whether one inbox carries both channels.** A LinkedIn-only inbox means a second tab open all day.
- **Whether warm-up is included.** Six of the eight have no warm-up product at all, so that becomes another subscription.
- **How many LinkedIn actions, and the daily caps.** Four actions is not the same product as seven.
- **What it costs at five seats, not one.** Every vendor quotes a headline price in the unit that flatters it.
- **What the one-star reviews cluster on.** G2 tags its own review set, so those counts are the platform's rather than mine.

One thing I did not do, since it matters for how you read the rest of this. I have not run 90-day tests on all eight tools.

Every price below came off the vendor's own live pricing page this week, on both billing cycles where the page shows both. Every rating came off the tool's own G2 page. Where a figure is not published, I say so instead of estimating it.
