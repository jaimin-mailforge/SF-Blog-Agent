# 9 Best Dripify Alternatives for LinkedIn + Email 2026

**Meta:** Dripify starts at $39 a seat billed annually, and one seat is one LinkedIn account. Here are 9 alternatives, priced at the seat count you actually buy.

---

Your reps are on Dripify Basic. Twenty connection requests a day, thirty messages, ten InMails, and once one of them hits that ceiling the sequence just waits. Then somebody hires a fourth rep and the invoice grows by $39 whether that rep touches LinkedIn or not.

That is what most people are trying to fix when they start looking at Dripify alternatives. It is rarely the sequence builder.

Dripify is a good LinkedIn drip tool. The campaign UI is clean, the seven actions cover what an SDR needs, and it is one of very few vendors that publishes per-plan daily quotas so nobody has to guess.

Three things send people looking anyway, and all three come off Dripify's own pages.

**The quota is the product.** The Basic ceiling of twenty connection requests a day is a different tool from the Pro ceiling of seventy-five, and most people comparing the $39 headline are picturing the Pro numbers.

**The seat buys one LinkedIn account.** Five reps means five seats, so five reps on Basic is $195 a month before anyone opens the app.

**And email sits outside the product.** Dripify has no warm up, no placement testing, and no unified inbox for both channels, so deliverability is your problem to solve elsewhere.

The G2 con tags back this up. **Expensive** is Dripify's top con tag at 37 mentions. **Email Limitations** follows at 11. Both are platform-counted, so they are not one reviewer's opinion.

So I screened 20 alternatives. Nine made the list. For each of those nine I read every price off the vendor's own [live pricing page](https://dripify.io/pricing), went through the product docs, and pulled the G2 star distribution.

The eleven that did not make it mostly failed on the same thing: LinkedIn only, with email arriving as a step inside a campaign rather than as a channel of its own.
